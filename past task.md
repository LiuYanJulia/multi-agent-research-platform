## Actual welcome-page instructions

### Your Task: Brainstorm New Ideas for a Career-Development Website

Please brainstorm new ideas for a career-development website and write a proposal presenting your ideas.

Many existing career-development websites offer familiar services, such as CV refinement and mock interviews. Your goal is to propose features, formats, or pipelines that could make a career-development website meaningfully different from what is already available.

Your proposal should introduce your ideas and explain how they would work. It will be evaluated on two criteria:

- **Creativity:** How original, distinctive, and imaginative are the ideas?
- **Usefulness:** How well could the ideas help users with their career development?

You may focus on new website features, end-to-end user journeys, interaction formats, or service pipelines. The final output should be a **proposal for new ideas for a career-development website**, clearly presenting the ideas and their potential value.

### Important reminder

Do not focus on technical details. Rather than a programmer or software engineer, please treat yourself as a **product manager**, to brainstorm creative and useful new ideas or features for the website, focusing on user needs, features, and experiences rather than implementation details.
